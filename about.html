Our story

Wellness in every bite

Poshaná is bringing customised, dietician-guided meal subscriptions to Bhimavaram — real food, cooked fresh, built around real goals.

Why Poshaná

Food that works as hard as you do

Poshaná started with a simple idea: healthy eating shouldn't mean choosing between convenience and nutrition. We pair every meal plan with real dietician consultation, so what arrives at your door isn't just food — it's a plan built for your body and your goals.

Bhimavaram is our first branch as we grow the Poshaná model city by city, bringing fresh, balanced, high-protein meals to communities that want better food, without the guesswork.

What drives us

Nourishing people. Building healthier communities.

Every plan we build, every meal we cook, comes back to one goal — helping people eat better, consistently, without it feeling like a chore.
